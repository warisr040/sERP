# sERP release notes

## 1.0 — November 2026
First release.
- **Accounting:** journal with balanced posting, drafts and reversals; chart of accounts in Thai and English; trial balance, profit & loss, balance sheet, tax summary; CSV export and print.
- **Sales & purchases:** invoices and bills with **VAT from the approved rule** in force on the document date; payments with **withholding tax from the approved rule** (income types, 1,000 baht minimum); partial payments.
- **Payroll:** draft → adjust → post; **social security and salary tax (PND1) from the rules** and each employee's approved tax profile; payslips show how the tax was calculated.
- **Tax rules:** versioned rules with effective dates; export/import as JSON or Excel with checks and a list of changes; maker-checker approval with configurable approver counts; back-dating policy with recalculation reports; change log.
- **Thai rule set:** VAT 7% to 30 Sep 2027 (Royal Decree 807), social security ceilings 2026–2032, PIT brackets and allowances, PND3/53 rates.
- **App:** five themes shared with sUML and sTrack; first-run setup that creates the sample database; connection tips; quick guide built in; keyboard shortcuts that work on Thai/English/Chinese/Japanese layouts.
- **Known limits:** no sign-in yet (acting user is chosen in the toolbar); back-dating adjustment entries are reported but not posted automatically.
